## Syncing Invoices and Bills with Xero

Export payment claims from PayTrade to Xero, or import invoices and bills from Xero into PayTrade. This guide covers both directions.

## Before You Begin

Before syncing invoices or bills, make sure:

- Your Xero integration is Active
- Account codes (invoice code, bill code, retention codes) are configured in "Xero Account Mapping and Settings"
- Tax rates are set for invoices and bills
- The relevant contact, project, and contract are all mapped (see "Mapping Bank Accounts, Contacts, Projects and Contracts in Xero")
Important: The system validates all mappings before syncing. If any contact, project, contract, or account code is missing, the sync will fail with a specific error in the Sync Log.

## PayTrade to Xero (Exporting Claims)

PayTrade payment claims are exported to Xero as either Invoices (receivable claims) or Bills (billable claims).

## How to Export

1Create a Payment Claim
Create or confirm a payment claim in PayTrade. Only claims with status "Draft" or "Confirmed" can be exported.

2Navigate to Xero Invoices or Bills
On the Xero Dashboard, click "View all" next to Invoices or Bills.

3Go to the PayTrade Tab
Click the "PayTrade Invoices" or "PayTrade Bills" tab to see claims ready for export.

4Click Sync to Xero
Select the claim and click "Sync to Xero". The system validates all mappings and creates the item in Xero.

5Verify on Mapped Tab
The synced item appears on the "Mapped" tab with links to both the PayTrade claim and the Xero record.

## What Gets Synced

 | PayTrade Field  | Xero Field  | Notes
 | Payment Claim ID  | Reference  | Prefixed with "Claim" or "Retention claim"
 | Claim Type  | Type  | Billable = ACCPAY (Bill), Receivable = ACCREC (Invoice)
 | Dates  | Date / Due Date  | Direction-dependent
 | Total (incl. GST)  | Unit Amount  | Adjusted by retention if applicable
 | Description  | Description  | Line item description
 | Project  | Tracking Category  | Uses project tracking category
 | Contract  | Tracking Category  | Uses contract tracking category

## Retention Handling on Export

When a claim includes retention, the system automatically splits the amount across multiple line items using the dedicated retention account codes configured in Settings. The main line item uses the invoice/bill code, and retention amounts use the retention payable/receivable codes.

## Xero to PayTrade (Importing Invoices/Bills)

## How to Import

1Create in Xero
Create an invoice or bill in Xero. Make sure it includes the correct tracking categories for both Project and Contract.

2Navigate to Xero Invoices or Bills
On the Xero Dashboard, click "View all" next to Invoices or Bills.

3Go to the Xero Tab
Click the "Xero Invoices" or "Xero Bills" tab to see items available for import.

4Click Sync to PayTrade
Select the item and click "Sync to PayTrade".

5Verify in PayTrade
The system creates a payment claim in PayTrade. Check the Claims list to see the imported claim.

## Import Validation

The system performs several checks before importing:

- At least one line item is required
- Valid tracking categories (Project and Contract) must be present on the invoice
- Account codes and tax codes must match your PayTrade configuration
- The total amount cannot exceed the contract size
- The invoice must not already be mapped to an existing PayTrade claim

If retention line items are present, the system back-calculates the original item prices and retention percentages automatically.

## Using Auto Map for Bulk Mapping

On the Mapped tab, click the "Auto Map" button to automatically match all unmapped invoices/bills by reference number. This is useful when you have many items to map at once.

## Unmap an Invoice or Bill

If an invoice or bill was incorrectly mapped:

1Go to the Mapped Tab
Navigate to Xero Invoices or Bills and click the Mapped tab.

2Click Unmap
Find the incorrect mapping and click "Unmap". The items return to their respective PayTrade or Xero tabs.

## Common Sync Errors

 | Error  | Cause  | Fix
 | Contact mismatch  | Invoice contact does not match mapped contact for the contract  | Update the contact mapping or fix the invoice in Xero
 | Project mismatch  | Invoice tracking category does not match mapped project  | Update the project mapping or fix the tracking category on the invoice
 | Contract mismatch  | Invoice contract tracking does not match mapped contract  | Update the contract mapping or fix the tracking category
 | Account code mismatch  | Invoice uses a different account code than configured  | Update the account code in Xero Settings or fix the invoice
 | Tax code mismatch  | Invoice uses a different tax rate than configured  | Update the tax code in Xero Settings or fix the invoice
 | S75 pending payment  | A bill has a pending payment and an invoice is created  | Fill in the reason for the pending payment in the error log
Tip: For detailed error resolution steps, see "Understanding Xero Sync Logs and Error Resolution".
