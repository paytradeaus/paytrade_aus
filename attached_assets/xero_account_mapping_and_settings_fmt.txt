## Xero Account Mapping and Settings

Configure your chart of accounts, tax rates, tracking categories, and sync preferences before syncing data between PayTrade and Xero.

Important: Complete these settings before attempting to sync invoices or bills. Missing account codes or tax rates will cause sync errors.

## Getting to Xero Settings

1Open the Xero Dashboard
Navigate to Integrations in the sidebar, then click on your Xero connection to open the Xero Dashboard.

2Click Settings
Click "Settings" on the Xero Dashboard or navigate directly to Xero Settings.

## Chart of Account Mappings

Map each PayTrade financial activity to a specific Xero account code. These codes tell Xero which ledger accounts to use when syncing.

 | Setting  | Purpose  | Example

 | Invoice Code  | Revenue account for receivable invoices  | 200 - Sales Revenue

 | Bill Code  | Expense account for payable bills  | 400 - Cost of Sales

 | Retention Payable Retained  | Account for retention amounts held (payable)  | 500 - Retention Held

 | Retention Payable Release  | Account for retention amounts released (payable)  | 502 - Retention Released

 | Retention Receivable Retained  | Account for retention amounts held (receivable)  | 503 - Retention Receivable Held

 | Retention Receivable Release  | Account for retention amounts released (receivable)  | 504 - Retention Receivable Released

 | Liability Payable Code  | Payable liabilities during defects period  | 501 - Current Liability

 | Liability Receivable Code  | Receivable liabilities during defects period  | 506 - Receivable Liability

Tip: If the account code you need doesn't exist in Xero, you can create a new Xero account directly from this screen by specifying the Account Type, Code, and Name.

## Tax Rate Configuration

1Set Invoice Tax Code
Choose the default tax rate for invoices synced to Xero (e.g., GST on Income 10%).

2Set Bill Tax Code
Choose the default tax rate for bills synced to Xero (e.g., GST on Expenses 10%).

You can also create new tax rates with custom components, compound tax support, and specific report tax types (Input, Output, None, Exempt).

## Tracking Categories

Tracking categories in Xero are used to categorise transactions by project and contract. PayTrade uses two:

1Project Tracking Category
Select the Xero tracking category that represents PayTrade projects. Each PayTrade project will map to an option within this category.

2Contract Tracking Category
Select the Xero tracking category that represents PayTrade contracts. Each contract will map to an option within this category.

Important: You cannot use the same tracking category for both projects and contracts. They must be different categories.
Tip: If the tracking category doesn't exist yet, you can create it directly from Settings.

## Sync Preferences

Control whether items arrive as Draft or Approved in each direction:

 | Direction  | Setting  | Options

 | PayTrade to Xero  | Invoice sync status  | Draft or Approved

 | PayTrade to Xero  | Bill sync status  | Draft or Approved

 | PayTrade to Xero  | Payment sync status  | Draft or Approved

 | Xero to PayTrade  | Invoice sync status  | Draft or Approved

 | Xero to PayTrade  | Bill sync status  | Draft or Approved

 | Xero to PayTrade  | Payment sync status  | Draft or Approved

Tip: Use Draft when you want to review synced items before they are finalised. Use Approved for a fully automated workflow.

## Other Settings

- Reference Format: Customise the reference prefix for synced documents (e.g., "PT-INV-" for invoices).

- Webhook Wait Time: Set a delay (0–60 minutes) before processing webhook events from Xero. A short delay batches rapid changes; setting it to 0 processes events immediately.

Note: Once all settings are configured, proceed to "Mapping Bank Accounts, Contacts, Projects and Contracts in Xero" to complete the onboarding wizard.
