## Mapping Bank Accounts, Contacts, Projects and Contracts in Xero

Link your Xero entities to their PayTrade counterparts. This is required before any invoice, bill, or payment can sync.

## How Entity Mapping Works

Each mapping screen follows the same three-tab pattern:

- Mapped Tab: Shows successfully linked entity pairs. Use "Auto Map" to automatically match by name, or "Unmap" to break a link.

- PayTrade Tab: Shows PayTrade entities that are not yet mapped. You can "Manual Map" to link to an existing Xero entity, or "Sync to Xero" to create the entity in Xero automatically.

- Xero Tab: Shows Xero entities not yet mapped. You can "Manual Map" to link to an existing PayTrade entity, or "Sync to PayTrade" to create the entity in PayTrade automatically.

Each tab includes a search field to quickly find specific entities.

## Bank Account Mapping

Navigate to Xero Bank Accounts on the Xero Dashboard.

1Review Your Xero Bank Accounts
The Xero tab lists all bank accounts from your Xero organisation.

2Map to PayTrade Accounts
For each Xero bank account, click "Manual Map" and select the corresponding PayTrade account — this can be a Cash Account, Project Trust Account (PTA), or Retention Trust Account (RTA).

3Or Use Auto Map
Click "Auto Map" on the Mapped tab to automatically match accounts by name.

4Or Sync New Accounts
If a Xero bank account has no PayTrade counterpart, click "Sync to PayTrade" to create one. If a PayTrade account has no Xero counterpart, click "Sync to Xero" to create one.

Important: Bank account mapping is critical for invoice and bill sync. The correct trust account must be linked before claims involving that account can sync.

## Contact Mapping

Navigate to Xero Contacts on the Xero Dashboard.

1Review Contacts
The Xero tab shows all contacts from Xero. The PayTrade tab shows all clients and suppliers from PayTrade.

2Map Contacts
For each contact, click "Manual Map" and select the matching client or supplier. Or use "Auto Map" to match by name.

3Import from Xero
Click "Sync to PayTrade" to create a new PayTrade client/supplier from a Xero contact.

4Export to Xero
Click "Sync to Xero" to create a new Xero contact from a PayTrade client/supplier.

Important: Contacts must be mapped before any invoice or bill referencing them can sync. If you see a sync error about a missing contact, check this mapping first.

## Project Mapping

Navigate to Xero Projects on the Xero Dashboard.

Note: Unlike bank accounts and contacts, projects map to tracking category options in Xero — not separate Xero entities. You must have a Project Tracking Category configured in Xero Settings first.
1Review Project Options
The Xero tab shows tracking category options from your configured Project Tracking Category.

2Map Projects
Link each Xero tracking category option to the corresponding PayTrade project.

3Create New Options
If a PayTrade project has no Xero counterpart, click "Sync to Xero" to create a new tracking category option.

## Contract Mapping

Navigate to Xero Contracts on the Xero Dashboard. The process is identical to Project Mapping but uses the Contract Tracking Category.

1Review Contract Options
The Xero tab shows tracking category options from your configured Contract Tracking Category.

2Map Contracts
Link each Xero tracking category option to the corresponding PayTrade contract.

3Create New Options
Use "Sync to Xero" or "Sync to PayTrade" as needed.

## The Onboarding Wizard

The Xero Dashboard presents these mappings as a 6-step onboarding wizard:

- Settings/Mapping — Chart of accounts, tax rates, tracking categories

- Bank Account Mapping

- Contact Mapping

- Project Tracking Category Mapping

- Contract Tracking Category Mapping

- Activate — Set the integration to Active status

Complete all six steps to activate your integration. You can return to any step later to update mappings.

## Handling Unmapped Entities

When the daily scheduler runs and finds new entities in Xero that aren't mapped:

 | New Entity In Xero  | What Happens

 | New Project  | Error log created. Click Resolve to navigate to the Project screen to create or map.

 | New Bank Account  | Error log created. Click Resolve to navigate to the Bank Account screen.

 | New Contact  | Error log created. Click Resolve to navigate to the Client/Supplier screen.

 | New Contract  | Error log created. Click Resolve to navigate to the Contract screen.

Tip: Check your Sync Log regularly after adding new projects, contracts, or contacts in Xero to ensure they are mapped promptly.
Note: Once all entities are mapped and the integration is activated, proceed to "Syncing Invoices and Bills with Xero" to start syncing data.
