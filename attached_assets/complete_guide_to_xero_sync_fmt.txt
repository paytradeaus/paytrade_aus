## Complete Guide to Xero Sync

A step-by-step walkthrough covering the entire Xero integration from connection to ongoing sync management. Follow this guide in order for initial setup, or jump to specific sections for reference.

Important: Xero integration is only available on Advanced and Pro Audit subscription plans. Upgrade your plan before proceeding.

## Overview: How Xero Sync Works

PayTrade's Xero integration provides two-way sync between your PayTrade and Xero accounts. Data flows in both directions:

- PayTrade to Xero: Payment claims, invoices, bills, and payments you create in PayTrade are exported to Xero as invoices, bills, and payments.

- Xero to PayTrade: Invoices, bills, and payments created in Xero are imported into PayTrade as payment claims and payments.

Sync happens in three ways: manually (you click Sync), automatically (daily scheduled sync at 1:00 PM UTC), and in real-time (Xero webhooks push changes as they happen).

## Step 1: Connect Xero

Follow the "Connecting Xero to PayTrade" guide for the initial OAuth connection. In summary:

1Navigate to Integrations
Go to Integrations in the sidebar menu.

2Click Connect Xero
Click the Xero connection button on the Integration List page.

3Authorise in Xero
Sign in to Xero and grant PayTrade access to your Xero organisation.

4Return to PayTrade
You will be redirected back to PayTrade automatically. Your Xero connection status will show as "Connected".

Note: PayTrade automatically refreshes your Xero access token every 23 hours. If your token expires (e.g., you revoked access in Xero), PayTrade will prompt you to re-authorise.

## Step 2: Configure Settings & Account Mappings

Follow the "Xero Account Mapping and Settings" guide for detailed instructions. Key tasks:

- Chart of Accounts: Map your PayTrade financial activities to Xero account codes (invoice code, bill code, retention codes, liability codes).

- Tax Rates: Set default invoice and bill tax codes for sync.

- Tracking Categories: Select which Xero tracking categories represent PayTrade projects and contracts.

- Sync Preferences: Choose whether synced items arrive as Draft or Approved in each direction.

- Reference Format: Customise the reference prefix for synced documents.

- Webhook Wait Time: Set how long the system waits before processing webhook events (0-60 minutes).

## Step 3: Map Bank Accounts, Contacts, Projects & Contracts

Follow the "Mapping Bank Accounts, Contacts, Projects and Contracts in Xero" guide for detailed instructions. This is the onboarding wizard:

1Map Bank Accounts
Link your Xero bank accounts to PayTrade Cash Accounts, Project Trust Accounts (PTA), or Retention Trust Accounts (RTA).

2Map Contacts
Link Xero contacts to PayTrade clients and suppliers. Contacts must be mapped before invoices referencing them can sync.

3Map Projects
Map a Xero tracking category to represent PayTrade projects. Each project maps to a tracking category option.

4Map Contracts
Map a Xero tracking category to represent PayTrade contracts. Each contract maps to a tracking category option.

5Activate
Once all mappings are complete, set the integration to Active status.

## Step 4: Sync Invoices and Bills

Follow the "Syncing Invoices and Bills with Xero" guide for the complete process.

## PayTrade to Xero (Export)

- Create a payment claim in PayTrade (receivable = invoice, billable = bill).

- Navigate to Xero Invoices or Xero Bills on the Xero Dashboard.

- Find the claim on the PayTrade tab and click "Sync to Xero".

- The system validates mappings (contact, project, contract, account codes) and creates the invoice/bill in Xero.

- The item appears on the Mapped tab with a link to the Xero record.

## Xero to PayTrade (Import)

- Create an invoice or bill in Xero with the correct tracking categories (project + contract).

- Navigate to Xero Invoices or Xero Bills on the Xero Dashboard.

- Find the item on the Xero tab and click "Sync to PayTrade".

- The system validates tracking categories and creates a payment claim in PayTrade.

- The item appears on the Mapped tab.

## Step 5: Sync Payments

Follow the "Xero Payment Synchronisation" guide for the complete process.

- PayTrade to Xero: Payments recorded against claims in PayTrade are exported to Xero as payments against the mapped invoice/bill.

- Xero to PayTrade: Payments recorded in Xero are imported into PayTrade as payment records against the mapped claim.

- Retention: Retention payments sync automatically with their own account codes.

- Special cases: Part payments, overpayments, credit notes, and pay-less-full scenarios are all handled automatically with appropriate error logs when manual action is needed.

## Step 6: Monitor Sync Activity

Follow the "Understanding Xero Sync Logs and Error Resolution" guide for detailed troubleshooting.

- The Xero Dashboard shows sync statistics: Synced vs. Pending counts for each entity type.

- The Sync Log table at the bottom shows all recent sync events with status (Succeeded, Warning, Failed).

- Click any log entry to see the full details including a side-by-side comparison of PayTrade and Xero records.

- Failed entries have a "Resolve" button that guides you to fix the issue.

## Ongoing Sync: What Happens Automatically

 | Mechanism  | When  | What It Does

 | Daily Scheduler  | 1:00 PM UTC daily  | Full refresh of accounts, contacts, projects, contracts, and invoices for all active integrations

 | Webhooks  | Real-time  | Xero sends events when contacts or invoices are created/updated. Events are queued and processed after the configured wait time.

 | Token Refresh  | Every 23 hours  | Keeps your Xero connection alive without manual re-authentication.

## Managing Your Connection

From the Integration List page, you can:

- Pause: Temporarily stop all sync without losing mappings. Useful during data cleanup.

- Unpause: Resume sync after a pause.

- Disconnect: Remove the Xero connection. Mappings are preserved and the integration moves to Archived.

- Delete: Permanently remove the integration and all mapping data.

- Re-connect: If Xero prompts for re-authentication, follow the OAuth flow again.
