## 1. Build `/project-trust-account` first

This is now the single highest-priority SEO task.

### Page title

**Project Trust Accounts Queensland: Complete 2026 Guide | PayTrade**

### H1

**Project Trust Accounts in Queensland**

Immediately underneath:

> A Project Trust Account (PTA) is a statutory trust account used for certain eligible Queensland construction contracts to protect progress payments owed to subcontractors. This guide explains when a PTA is required, how it works, who operates it, how payments must be handled and the records trustees need to maintain.

Then put an extremely visible:

**Current rules — reviewed 14 August 2026**

And explain the present thresholds.

The current Queensland Government position is that the framework applies to eligible Queensland Government contracts of **$1 million+** and private-sector, local-government, statutory-authority and government-owned-corporation contracts of **$10 million+**. The planned reductions to $3m and then $1m were paused. ([housing.qld.gov.au][3])

This is an important current-information advantage.

# 3. Change how the Community works

This is probably the second most important change.

Google is indexing a lot of PayTrade community content already. Recent community discussions are appearing in search within days. ([Paytrade][6])

That's good.

But don't allow the community to become your canonical knowledge base.

I would change the content system so every new community article is automatically classified against the canonical topic map.

For example:

**Community question**

> How often should I reconcile a Project Trust Account?

The community page answers the specific question.

But immediately underneath:

> **Complete guide:** Project Trust Account Reconciliation

→ `/project-trust-account/reconciliation`

And the canonical reconciliation guide itself links to selected useful community discussions.

### Very important

Run every existing community URL through a content-quality process:

**KEEP** — unique useful question.

**UPDATE** — useful but factually outdated.

**MERGE** — substantially duplicates another page.

**NOINDEX** — low-value/near-duplicate generated content.

Do **not** simply delete indexed pages en masse.

And don't use canonical tags to point unrelated questions at a guide merely because they're about the same subject. Canonicals should represent genuinely duplicate/substantially equivalent content.

# 11. Technical implementation

I'd have the Replit agent make the following site-wide architecture changes.

Use:

**Organization structured data**

on the core entity pages.

**SoftwareApplication**

on the actual PayTrade software/product pages. Google explicitly supports `SoftwareApplication` markup. ([Google for Developers][11])

**Article**

on substantial informational guides. ([Google for Developers][12])

**BreadcrumbList**

through the guide structure. ([Google for Developers][13])

And make sure structured-data claims exactly match visible page content. Google specifically requires this. ([Google for Developers][14])

I would **not spend much engineering time on FAQ schema**.

FAQ sections themselves are valuable, but Google's FAQ rich-result treatment is now heavily restricted. The text matters more than trying to manufacture rich results.

And one correction to something PayTrade is currently emphasising:

### Don't treat `llms.txt` as a Google GEO lever.

You can leave it there — other systems may choose to use it — but Google's 2026 documentation explicitly says **Google Search does not need or use special `llms.txt`/AI files to qualify for generative Search**. ([Google for Developers][1])

Your engineering effort is much better spent on the content itself.
