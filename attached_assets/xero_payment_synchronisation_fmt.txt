## Xero Payment Synchronisation

How payments sync between PayTrade and Xero, including retention, overpayments, credit notes, and error resolution for special payment scenarios.

## How Payment Sync Works

Payments in PayTrade and Xero are linked through the mapped invoices and bills they apply to. When a payment is recorded against a mapped invoice/bill in either system, it can be synced to the other.

- Payments follow the same Draft or Approved sync preference as invoices and bills.
- The daily scheduler and webhooks automatically detect new payments.
- You can also sync payments manually from the Xero Dashboard.

## PayTrade to Xero (Exporting Payments)

1Record a Payment in PayTrade
From the claim view page, click "Add Payment", enter the payment details, and save.

2Navigate to Xero Payments
On the Xero Dashboard, click "View all" next to Payments.

3Go to the PayTrade Payments Tab
Find the payment and click "Sync to Xero".

4Verify
The payment appears on the "Mapped Payments" tab and is applied against the corresponding invoice/bill in Xero.

Important: The billable claim must be under "Add Payment" status for the bill payment to sync to Xero.

## Xero to PayTrade (Importing Payments)

1Record a Payment in Xero
Apply a payment to an invoice or bill in Xero.

2Navigate to Xero Payments
On the Xero Dashboard, go to the Payments section.

3Go to the Xero Payments Tab
Find the payment and click "Sync to PayTrade". Or use "Manual Map" to link to an existing PayTrade payment.

4Verify
The payment is recorded in PayTrade against the mapped claim.

Important: The claim must be under "Confirm paid - matched" status for the payment to sync from Xero to PayTrade.

## Retention Payments

When a claim with retention is paid:

- The system automatically creates a retention record in the Retention List.
- Retention amounts sync using the dedicated retention account codes configured in Xero Settings.
- When the retention is later released, it follows the same sync process.

## Special Payment Scenarios

## Part Payments and Pay-Less-Full

When a partial payment is made in Xero:

- A $500 part payment is recorded in Xero against a $1,000 bill.
- PayTrade imports this as a "part payment".
- If the remaining $500 is later cleared using a credit note in Xero, the payment type changes to "pay less full".
- PayTrade detects the mismatch and triggers an "unconfirm payment" error in the Sync Log.
- Click "Resolve" to unconfirm the old payment. PayTrade then deletes the old record and creates a new "pay less full" record.
- The original part payment notice is archived and a new notice is generated for the pay-less-full payment type.

## Withholding Payment Reason

When a payment is recorded as Part, Payless, Pay Zero, or Third Party in Xero:

- PayTrade triggers a "Withholding reason" error in the Sync Log.
- Click "Resolve" to navigate to the payment screen.
- Enter the reason for withholding payment.
- Once the reason is saved, the payment is recorded in PayTrade and journals/notices are generated.
Note: The withholding reason error is triggered based on the schedule setting timing configured in Xero Settings (Webhook Wait Time).

## Overpayments

When a payment exceeding the invoice/bill amount is made in Xero:

- Example: Invoice is $1,000 but payment of $1,200 is recorded. Xero applies $1,000 and creates a $200 overpayment.
- PayTrade detects the discrepancy and creates an "overpayment missing" error in the Sync Log.
- Click "Resolve" to open the Map Claim/Payment screen.
- Map the overpayment to the correct claim. An overpayment record is saved in the Payment List.

## Underpayments and Credit Application

When an overpayment credit from one bill is applied to another:

- Example: Bill 1 ($1,000) receives $1,200 payment, creating a $200 credit.
- Bill 2 ($500) receives $200 from the credit + $300 new payment = $500 total.
- PayTrade detects the mixed payment source and creates an "underpayment" error.
- Click "Resolve" to map the credit application correctly.

## Payment Edited in Xero

If a synced payment is edited in Xero (e.g., amount changed from $1,000 to $800):

- PayTrade creates an error log for the payment edit.
- Click "Resolve" to navigate to the Payment screen.
- Unconfirm the payment by clearing the "Confirm paid" checkbox.
- PayTrade updates the payment to match the edited amount in Xero.

## Payment Deleted in Xero

If a synced payment is deleted in Xero:

- PayTrade creates an error stating the payment type cannot be deleted.
- Click "Resolve".
- If the payment is matched to a bank transaction, you are taken to the Transaction Unmatch screen first. Unmatch the transaction.
- Then unconfirm and delete the payment in PayTrade.

## Auto Map for Payments

Use the "Auto Map" button on the Mapped Payments tab to automatically match payments by reference. Use "Unmap" to break incorrect payment mappings.

Tip: For a complete view of all sync events, see "Understanding Xero Sync Logs and Error Resolution".
